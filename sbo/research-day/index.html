Research Day: How Do You Decide What to Charge?

You need: your Calumet Lawn & Snow case file

Calumet has not changed its prices since 2022. Before an owner changes a price, the owner needs evidence. But many lawn companies do not post their prices. Today you will find out how owners decide what to charge. You will use your answers in a later module.

Write your answers in your own words. There are 10 questions. Each one is worth 2 points: 2 = right and complete, 1 = partly right, 0 = missing or wrong.

Words for today

  • Quote or bid: the price a company offers before it does the work.
  • Per cut: the price for mowing one time.
  • Man hour: one worker working for one hour. Two workers for one hour is two man hours.
  • Contract: a written agreement between a business and a customer.
