How Your Work Is Graded

All three courses · Every module: Part B Practical Application — 25 pts · Checkout — 50 pts · Quiz — 10 pts (in Formative)

Read these before you turn anything in. These two rubrics score every module all year. Part A is worth 0 points but is required — it unlocks Part B and it's where your checkout answers come from.

Practical Application Rubric — Part B (25 points)

Criteria4.0 — Exemplary3.0 — Proficient2.0 — Developing1.0 — Beginning
Technical Execution & Accuracy
10 pts		All required components completed with meticulous accuracy and attention to detail. Calculations are flawless; templates used perfectly. 10All required components completed with only minor errors that don't affect the overall outcome. 8Most components completed, but noticeable errors or omissions impact the quality. 6Significant portions incomplete or containing fundamental errors. 0–5
Application of Concepts
5 pts		Deep understanding of the module's core concepts, applied in a way that is insightful and effective. 5Solid understanding of the core concepts, applied correctly to the task. 4Partial understanding; application is inconsistent or flawed. 3Significant misunderstanding of the module's core concepts. 0–2
Creativity & Initiative
5 pts		Meets requirements and shows real originality or initiative — insightful details, connections beyond the prompt. 5Meets all requirements in a competent, thoughtful way with solid effort. 4Meets the basic requirements but lacks detail, creativity, or proactive effort. 3Superficial; bare minimum, if that. 0–2
Professionalism & Clarity
5 pts		Exceptionally clear, well-organized, professionally formatted — very easy to understand. 5Clear, organized, easy to follow. 4Somewhat disorganized or unclear; the reader has to guess. 3Disorganized, hard to read, unprofessional appearance. 0–2

Checkout Rubric — the conversation with Mr. Clark (50 points)

The checkout is a one-on-one conversation about YOUR work. A missed question doesn't end it — you go study that item, come back, and re-answer. You're done when you've shown you own the material.

Criteria4.0 — Exemplary3.0 — Proficient2.0 — Developing1.0 — Beginning
Content Knowledge & Recall
15 pts		Mastery — recalls specific data and concepts from your practical work instantly and accurately. 14–15Solid command — recalls key data and concepts with minimal prompting or error. 12–13Recalls some key information but struggles with specifics; needs prompting on your own work. 10–11Unable to recall most key data or concepts from your practical work. 0–9
Strategic Thinking & Justification
15 pts		Clear, logical, insightful justifications — analyzes your work, spots challenges, proposes thoughtful next steps. 14–15Clear, logical justifications — explains the "why" behind your work. 12–13Can describe what you did but struggles to justify the choices. 10–11Can't explain the reasoning behind your work. 0–9
Clarity & Articulation
10 pts		Exceptional clarity, confidence, precision — professional language, easy to follow. 10Clear and effective — answers directly, easily understood. 8Sometimes unclear, disorganized, or hard to follow. 6–7Largely unable to articulate; confusing or incoherent. 0–5
Poise & Professionalism
10 pts		Confident, composed, professional — mature dialogue, handles questions with ease. 10Composed and professional — listens actively, answers respectfully. 8Nervousness or unprofessionalism gets in the way of communicating. 6–7Disengaged, unprofessional, or unable to handle the back-and-forth. 0–5
